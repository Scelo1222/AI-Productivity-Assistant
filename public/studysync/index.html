Responsible AI use

  • StudySync AI produces drafts and study aids, never finished submissions.
  • Always fact-check summaries, research points and citations yourself.
  • Submitting AI text as your own work may breach your school's academic-integrity policy.
  • Everything you type stays in your browser (localStorage) — nothing is uploaded.
  • AI is not a substitute for a lecturer, doctor or counsellor.
